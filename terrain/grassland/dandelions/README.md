`dandelions.glb` is 205,668 bytes: four plants, one shared material and three
embedded source images (base colour/alpha, packed ORM, normal). Geometry, UVs,
normals and images are unchanged. Ground checker, reference person, lettuce,
plantain and redundant Dandelion.004 are omitted. Display translations are
cleared, ancestor rotations/scales retained; load the complete hierarchy before
baking a prototype. Raw geometry uses Z up; complete glTF loading gives Y up.

001 is compact and upright; 002 has a wider, leaning flower silhouette; 003
has fuller foliage and flowers at different heights. 005 supplies unopened buds.
These are yellow-flower plants, not white seed-head dandelions. No source LODs
exist; these meshes are already economical. The proposed mix averages 43.6
triangles per flowering plant.

The source material was double-sided alpha BLEND; this preparation changes it
to double-sided MASK with cutoff 0.35. Source textures and metallic factor 0.01
are retained. Native Chrome/WebGPU preview checked visible leaves/flowers and
loading without GPU errors. See [preview.png](preview.png). This is an asset
preview, not a production lighting or custom depth-pass validation.

`dandelions.manifest.json` records source SHA256, provenance, natural heights
and ground offsets. Ground offsets must be applied after hierarchy baking and
scaled with the instance: the original minimum extends 8-13 mm below the pivot.
Keep roots anchored on terrain triangles; do not ground on the flower bounds.

Reproduce the GLB and manifest with `node scripts/prepare-dandelions.mjs`.
The preview is a native WebGPU screenshot, not generated by the extractor.
The three flowering variants now populate the authored main-app grass biome.
005 remains an optional prepared accent. Runtime details are in GRASS.md.

## Suggested production placement

- Start at The Long Green with irregular colonies, not uniform coverage:
  3-8 plants per colony, 1-3 m radius, covering about 5-10% of eligible meadow.
  Use deterministic coarse noise, jitter, yaw and the 45/35/20 flowering mix.
- Prefer open meadow and occasional road verges. Exclude the road surface,
  non-grass biomes, underwater terrain and steep slopes. Use the same ground
  coverage and actual triangle sampling as production grass.
- Trial 0.3-0.4 m heights and locally reduce nearby grass slightly. Natural
  24 cm plants can disappear behind current grass; avoid enormous flowers.
  Keep the surrounding full grass density away from flower colonies.
- Stream in existing 16 m patch coordinates, with independent density and
  budgets. Batch by model/shared material, use camera-frustum rejection and
  fade between 35 and 50 m. Start with shadows off and no collision.
- Add matching alpha sampling/cutoff to production colour and camera-depth
  passes, and warm both before gameplay. Existing grass uses opaque geometry;
  importing its material unchanged would leave rectangular leaf cards.
  Reuse gentle wind and root-anchored walking/surf response with identical
  deformation in both passes. No new particle effect is proposed.
- Benchmark grass-only against grass plus flowers on the same automatic route
  before increasing coverage. Low triangle counts do not eliminate alpha-card
  overdraw or draw submission cost.
